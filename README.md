# Mehrad Yaghoubi
### AI researcher and engineer · Visual Speech Recognition · Computer Vision

I am an AI Specialist and M.Sc. researcher in Artificial Intelligence at Islamic Azad University, Karaj. My research centers on **visual speech recognition (lip reading)**, **knowledge distillation**, **uncertainty-aware learning**, and efficient deep learning.

I build practical AI systems as well as research prototypes, with a focus on clear documentation and reproducible work.

## Research

My current research explores how compact lip-reading models can learn effectively from stronger models while accounting for uncertainty.

- [Entropy-gated online self-distillation for lightweight lip reading](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6831426) — preprint; under review at *The Visual Computer* (status as reported in my CV).
- [Uncertainty-gated, belief-guided decision-making for Go](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26946) — preprint.

## Applied AI and engineering

At Ghasedak Peik Shahr, I build AI and web tools for travel services. Recent work includes a Persian-language booking chatbot and WordPress integrations for Parsegasht and TaxiRooz, a competitor price-checking bot, an operator training dashboard, and a custom homepage theme.

I have also built an API-based OCR application. A personal retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) chatbot is in development.
